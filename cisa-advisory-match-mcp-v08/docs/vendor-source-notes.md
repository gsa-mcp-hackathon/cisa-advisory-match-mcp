Search result excerpts include the relevant name/relationship text. Names are exact curated entries; no suffix-stripping or general transliteration rule is authorized by this dataset. These records establish manufacturer naming or corporate relationships only, never product/version applicability.

`equivalent` records are manufacturer-field naming equivalences in the declared ICS context. `candidate` records only help discovery or clarification; they must not be promoted to identity or affected status. Candidate relationships must not form transitive equivalence classes.

## Deliberate exclusions and cautions

- WAGO and Pilz: attempted official legal-page reads failed, and search results chiefly exposed third-party copies. No record was added from an inaccessible official page.
- Delta: search surfaced separate unrelated Delta Electronics businesses. Bare Delta is ambiguous; no equivalence was added.
- GE/Emerson: public secondary reports were retrieved, but the requested official acquisition source was not resolved in this pass. No record added.
- Mitsubishi alone, Bosch alone, Phoenix alone, and unverified A-B/AB abbreviations are omitted because they are broad or ambiguous.
- Weidmueller transliteration is explicitly scoped to one listed name group; it does not justify removing diacritics globally.
- Tridium Inc. versus Tridium, Inc.: the former exact form is in the vendor about page. Comma variant was not added without a separately scoped source.
- Company names and relationships can change. Sources were inspected for this revision; no automatic freshness monitoring is established.
- PTC/Kepware is explicitly historical in light of the March 16, 2026 divestiture. AVEVA/Wonderware is a scoped portfolio lead evidenced by the System Platform document, not a universal product rename.
- Official corporate pages support trade-name/legal-name mapping, but do not prove a particular label is present in the CISA CSAF corpus; that is a separate source-corpus test.
